# Government social security & financial inclusion schemes available at all banks

=== PRODUCT

---
Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) account under the National Mission for Financial Inclusion, available at every bank branch and Business Correspondent (BC) outlet.
- Zero balance; no minimum balance charges.
- Free RuPay debit card with built-in accidental insurance of ₹2 lakh (for accounts opened after 28 Aug 2018).
- Overdraft facility up to ₹10,000 for eligible account holders (satisfactory operation for 6 months; no conditions up to ₹2,000).
- Receives Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) from government schemes.
- Gateway to PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY and Mudra.
- Small accounts can be opened with self-certified KYC if no officially valid documents are available (with restrictions).
Best fit: unbanked or low-income clients, daily-wage workers, rural customers, DBT beneficiaries. Cross-sell PMJJBY + PMSBY + APY at the same visit.

---
Government-backed one-year renewable term life insurance offered through every bank via auto-debit from the savings account.
- Eligibility: age 18–50 with a savings bank account and consent for auto-debit (cover continues up to 55 if enrolled before 50).
- Cover: ₹2 lakh on death due to any cause.
- Premium: ₹436 per year (pro-rata for mid-year enrolment); cover period 1 June–31 May.
- Lien period of 30 days from enrolment for non-accidental deaths.
- Enrolment via branch, net banking or mobile banking (YONO, HDFC, ICICI iMobile etc.).
Best fit: any account holder 18–50 without term insurance; an easy "add-on" at account opening.

---
One-year renewable accidental death and disability cover available through every bank.
- Eligibility: age 18–70 with a savings bank account.
- Cover: ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total permanent disability; ₹1 lakh for partial permanent disability.
- Premium: ₹20 per year, auto-debited; cover period 1 June–31 May.
Best fit: everyone 18–70 — at ₹20/year it is the easiest cross-sell. Pair with PMJJBY.

---
Government-guaranteed pension scheme for the unorganised sector, administered by PFRDA and offered through all banks.
- Eligibility: Indian citizens aged 18–40 with a savings account. Since 1 Oct 2022, income-tax payers are not eligible.
- Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000 or ₹5,000 from age 60, depending on contribution.
- Contribution depends on joining age and chosen pension (e.g., joining at 18 for ₹5,000 pension ≈ ₹210/month; later joining costs more) — monthly, quarterly or half-yearly auto-debit.
- After the subscriber's death, the spouse gets the same pension; after both, the nominee receives the accumulated corpus.
Best fit: young workers in the unorganised sector (drivers, domestic workers, small shopkeepers, farm workers) who don't pay income tax. Earlier joining = lower contribution — a strong urgency message.

# Deposit and account products published by Indian banks
# Bank deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal + interest).

=== PRODUCT
id: fd-generic
title: Fixed Deposits (Term Deposits) — SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak, PNB, BoB, Canara
bank: Generic
category: deposit
segments: individual, senior_citizen, conservative, hni, minor, corporate, msme, nri
tags: fixed deposit, FD, guaranteed returns, safe, cumulative, monthly interest, loan against FD, DICGC
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/interest-rates/deposit-rates/retail-domestic-term-deposits
---
- Tenure 7 days to 10 years; cumulative (reinvestment) or periodic payout (monthly/quarterly) options.
- Senior citizens (60+) usually get 0.25%–0.50% extra; some banks offer a further premium for super seniors (80+) or special tenures.
- Banks periodically launch special-tenure FDs (e.g., 300–500 day buckets) with higher rates — check the current rate card.
- Loan/overdraft against FD up to ~90% of deposit value.
- Premature withdrawal allowed with a penalty (non-callable bulk FDs excluded).
- Interest taxable as per slab; TDS above the threshold (higher threshold for senior citizens); Form 15G/15H if eligible.
- Insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank by DICGC.
Best fit: risk-averse clients, retirees, corporates parking surplus; any client who wants a fixed, guaranteed return.

=== PRODUCT
id: tax-saver-fd
title: Tax Saver Fixed Deposit (5-year, Section 80C)
bank: Generic
category: deposit
segments: individual, salaried, senior_citizen, conservative
tags: tax saving, 80C, 5 year lock in, guaranteed returns
source: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/fixed-deposits/tax-saving-fixed-deposit
---
Offered by all banks (SBI Tax Savings Scheme, HDFC Bank Tax Saver FD, ICICI Tax Saver FD etc.).
- Deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80C (old tax regime only).
- Mandatory 5-year lock-in; no premature withdrawal or loan against it.
- Interest taxable.
Best fit: conservative tax-payers on the old regime who want guaranteed returns instead of ELSS market risk.

=== PRODUCT

bank: Generic
category: deposit
segments: individual, salaried, women, student, low_income, minor
tags: monthly saving, small amount, goal saving, disciplined saving, guaranteed
source: https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/deposits/recurring-deposit
---
- Fixed monthly instalment (from ₹100 at many banks) for 6 months to 10 years, earning FD-equivalent rates.
- Auto-debit from savings account; loan against RD available.
- Some banks offer flexi/goal-based RDs via app (e.g., iWish at ICICI).
Best fit: salaried clients saving for a goal (vacation, gadget, down payment); first-time savers; youngsters starting a saving habit.

=== PRODUCT
id: sweep-in-mod
title: Auto-sweep / Multi Option Deposit — SBI MOD, HDFC Bank Sweep-in (Super Saver), ICICI Money Multiplier, Kotak ActivMoney
bank: Generic
category: deposit
segments: individual, salaried, hni, msme, self_employed
tags: sweep in, liquidity, higher interest on savings, idle balance, auto FD
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/accounts/term-deposit/multi-option-deposit-scheme
---
- Surplus above a threshold in the savings/current account is automatically swept into an FD earning higher interest.
- When the client needs money, funds are reverse-swept from the FD in units, so liquidity is the same as savings.
Best fit: clients who keep large idle balances in savings/current accounts; business owners with fluctuating cash.

=== PRODUCT
id: current-account-generic
title: Current Accounts for businesses — SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak
bank: Generic
category: account
segments: msme, self_employed, startup, corporate, trader, professional
tags: current account, business account, unlimited transactions, cash deposit, GST, merchant, startup account
source: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/current-accounts
---
- Variants based on average quarterly/monthly balance with matching free cash-deposit limits and transaction bundles (e.g., trader, startup, professional, e-commerce seller variants).
- Free NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, multi-city cheques, bulk payments, GST payments, POS/QR collection integration.

Best fit: any business client; pick the variant by their monthly cash deposit volume and transaction pattern.

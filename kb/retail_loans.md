# Retail loan products published by Indian banks
# Most retail floating-rate loans are linked to the bank's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR, usually repo-linked) or MCLR. Pricing depends on CIBIL score, income and LTV — quote the current rate card.

title: SBI Regular Home Loan (with Privilege, Shaurya and top-up variants)
bank: SBI
category: loan
segments: individual, salaried, self_employed, govt_employee, defence, women, nri, first_time_home_buyer
tags: home loan, house purchase, construction, EBLR, long tenure, tax benefit, 24b, 80C, women concession
source: https://homeloans.sbi
---
State Bank of India home loans for purchase, construction, extension or repair of a house.
- Tenure up to 30 years (repayment must finish by age 70/75 as per policy).
- Floating rate linked to SBI's EBLR; spread depends on CIBIL score. Concession for women borrowers.
- No pre-payment penalty on floating-rate individual home loans (RBI rule).
- Variants: SBI Privilege Home Loan (central/state government employees incl. PSUs, banks), SBI Shaurya Home Loan (defence personnel), SBI Top-Up Loan (on existing home loan), SBI Realty (plot purchase), SBI NRI Home Loan, SBI Tribal Plus, SBI MaxGain (home loan as overdraft — park surplus to save interest).
- Processing fee as per offer; frequent festive waivers.
- Tax (old regime): interest up to ₹2 lakh under Sec 24(b) for self-occupied property; principal under 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh. Joint co-owner borrowers can each claim.
Best fit: salaried/government employees and defence personnel buying a home; clients who want the comfort of the largest PSU bank. Pitch MaxGain to clients with large surplus balances.

=== PRODUCT

title: Loan Against Property (LAP) — SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak

tags: mortgage loan, secured loan, business expansion, debt consolidation, lower interest than personal loan, overdraft

---
Secured loan against residential or commercial property, offered by all major banks (e.g., SBI Property Loan, HDFC Bank LAP, ICICI LAP, Axis Bank LAP).
- Loan amount typically 50%–70% of property market value (LTV varies by property type).
- Tenure usually up to 15 years; term loan or overdraft variant.
- Much cheaper than personal or unsecured business loans.
- End use: business expansion, working capital, children's education/wedding, debt consolidation (not speculative).
Best fit: self-employed business owners and professionals who own property and need large funds at lower rates; clients consolidating high-cost credit card/personal loan debt.

Loan against gold jewellery/ornaments, disbursed same day with minimal documentation.
- LTV caps per RBI's 2025 gold-loan directions: up to 85% of gold value for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for ₹2.5–5 lakh, 75% above ₹5 lakh (verify current RBI norms).

- Agricultural gold loans for farmers at concessional rates (with land records).
- Typically cheaper than personal loans; no income proof needed in many cases.
Best fit: clients needing quick short-term funds who hold gold — farmers before sowing, small traders, medical emergencies.

=== PRODUCT
id: sbi-education-loans
title: SBI Education Loans — Scholar Loan, Student Loan, Global Ed-Vantage
bank: SBI
category: loan
segments: student, individual, salaried
tags: education loan, study abroad, premier institutes, IIT, IIM, collateral free, 80E tax benefit
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/personal-banking/loans/education-loans
---
- SBI Scholar Loan: for students admitted to premier institutes (IITs, IIMs, NITs, AIIMS, top NIRF institutes) — collateral-free up to high limits, concessional rate.
- SBI Student Loan Scheme: for higher education in India and abroad; collateral needed above specified limits.
- SBI Global Ed-Vantage: for studying abroad (full-time regular courses); higher loan amounts against collateral; can be sanctioned before I-20/visa.
- Moratorium: course period + 12 months; repayment up to 15 years.
- Interest on education loans is deductible under Section 80E for 8 years (no upper limit; old and new regime differences apply — 80E is available only in old regime).
- Applications also through the Vidya Lakshmi / PM Vidyalaxmi portals.
Best fit: parents of students admitted to premier Indian institutes or foreign universities.

tags: reverse mortgage, monthly income, own house, retirement income, no EMI

---
- For senior citizens (60+; spouse 58+ for joint) who own a self-occupied residential house.
- Bank pays the borrower periodic instalments or a lump sum against the house; no EMIs; loan recovered from sale of property after the death of the last surviving borrower (heirs can repay and retain the house).
- Tenure up to 15 years; amount based on property value and age.
Best fit: asset-rich, cash-poor retired clients who own a house but need regular income.

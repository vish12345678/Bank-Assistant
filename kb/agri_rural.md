# Agriculture and rural banking products

=== PRODUCT
id: kcc
title: Kisan Credit Card (KCC) — crop loan with interest subvention
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: farmer, tenant_farmer, rural, sharecropper
tags: crop loan, short term agri credit, 4 percent effective interest, interest subvention, MISS, collateral free, PMFBY
source: https://www.nabard.org/content1.aspx?id=572
---
Offered by SBI, PNB, BoB, Canara, Union Bank, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, RRBs and cooperative banks.
- Revolving credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, farm maintenance and household consumption; limit based on scale of finance × acreage, valid 5 years.
- Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS): 7% interest with 3% prompt repayment incentive = effective 4% p.a. — limit under MISS raised from ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh in Union Budget 2025-26 (verify notification).
- Collateral-free up to ₹2 lakh (RBI raised from ₹1.6 lakh from Jan 2025).
- RuPay KCC debit card; crop insurance under PMFBY.
- Eligible: owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, SHGs/JLGs of farmers.
Best fit: any farmer needing seasonal crop finance. Ask about landholding, crops and existing KCC.

=== PRODUCT
id: kcc-animal-fisheries
title: KCC for Animal Husbandry and Fisheries
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: farmer, dairy_farmer, fisherman, rural
tags: dairy, poultry, fisheries, livestock, working capital, interest subvention
source: https://www.dahd.gov.in
---
- Working capital for dairy, poultry, sheep/goat rearing, pig farming and fisheries/aquaculture.
- Same interest subvention benefits as crop KCC (combined limits); collateral-free up to RBI's limit.
Best fit: dairy farmers, poultry farmers and fishermen needing feed/fodder/maintenance working capital.

=== PRODUCT
id: farm-mechanisation
title: Farm mechanisation loans — tractor, harvester, implements (SBI Stree Shakti Tractor Loan, SBI Tractor Loan, BoB, PNB, HDFC Bank tractor loans)
bank: Generic
category: loan
segments: farmer, rural, agri_entrepreneur
tags: tractor loan, harvester, farm equipment, drip irrigation, solar pump
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/agri-rural/agriculture-banking/farm-mechanisation-loan
---
- Term loans for tractors, power tillers, harvesters and implements; repayment aligned with harvest cycles (half-yearly/annual instalments); tenure up to 5–9 years.
- Also financing for minor irrigation (drip/sprinkler, bore wells) and solar pumps under PM-KUSUM.
Best fit: farmers with adequate landholding seeking to mechanise; custom hiring centre entrepreneurs.

=== PRODUCT
id: shg-bank-linkage
title: Self Help Group (SHG) bank linkage loans under DAY-NRLM
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: women, rural, low_income, shg
tags: SHG, women groups, collateral free, interest subvention, livelihood, Lakhpati Didi
source: https://aajeevika.gov.in
---
- Savings-linked loans to women SHGs (usually 10–20 members) for income-generating activities.
- Collateral-free loans up to ₹20 lakh per SHG (no margin/collateral); repeat doses in increasing size.
- Interest subvention under DAY-NRLM bringing effective rates down for eligible SHGs (category-wise), plus additional subvention on prompt repayment.
Best fit: rural women's groups; strong financial-inclusion and priority-sector lending target.

=== PRODUCT
id: warehouse-receipt-finance
title: Warehouse receipt / Negotiable Warehouse Receipt (e-NWR) finance
bank: Generic
category: loan
segments: farmer, fpo, trader, agri_entrepreneur
tags: post harvest, pledge loan, avoid distress sale, WDRA, e-NWR, commodity finance
source: https://wdra.gov.in
---
- Loans against produce stored in WDRA-registered warehouses using electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts.
- Lets farmers avoid distress sales at harvest and sell when prices improve; post-harvest loans to KCC holders get interest subvention for up to 6 months.
- Credit Guarantee Scheme for e-NWR based pledge financing (launched 2024).
Best fit: farmers/FPOs and traders holding produce after harvest.

# MSME and corporate banking products published by Indian banks

=== PRODUCT
id: working-capital-cc-od
title: Working capital — Cash Credit (CC) and Overdraft (OD) limits for MSMEs
bank: Generic
category: loan
segments: msme, trader, manufacturer, self_employed, corporate
tags: working capital, cash credit, overdraft, inventory, receivables, stock statement, drawing power
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/sme/sme-loans
---
Offered by SBI (SME Smart Score, General Purpose Term Loan, Open CC), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, BoB, PNB, Canara, Kotak.
- Revolving limit against hypothecation of stock and book debts; interest only on amount used.
- Drawing power based on monthly stock/debtors statements; annual renewal.
- Limits up to ₹10 crore can be collateral-free with CGTMSE cover for micro & small enterprises.
- Many banks offer GST-return-based or cash-flow-based assessment for small limits (e.g., digital MSME loans with in-principle sanction in minutes).
Best fit: traders, distributors and manufacturers with inventory/receivable cycles.

=== PRODUCT
id: msme-term-loan
title: MSME Term Loans — machinery, expansion, business premises
bank: Generic
category: loan
segments: msme, manufacturer, self_employed, startup, corporate
tags: term loan, machinery loan, capex, expansion, factory, equipment finance
source: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/business-loan
---
- Term loans for purchase of machinery, equipment, vehicles, business premises or expansion; tenure 3–10 years with moratorium.
- Secured by the asset financed plus collateral, or CGTMSE cover up to ₹10 crore.
- Unsecured business loans (HDFC Bank, ICICI, Axis, Kotak) for established businesses based on turnover and ITR, typically 1–4 years.
- Combine with PMEGP subsidy (new units) or Stand-Up India (SC/ST/women) where eligible.
Best fit: growing MSMEs needing capex funding.

=== PRODUCT
id: treds
title: TReDS invoice discounting (RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart) via bank financiers
bank: Generic
category: loan
segments: msme, manufacturer, supplier, corporate
tags: invoice discounting, receivables, bill discounting, without recourse, large buyer, delayed payments
source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=TReDS
---
- RBI-regulated platforms where MSME suppliers upload invoices raised on corporate/PSU/government buyers; banks bid to discount them.
- Financing is "without recourse" to the MSME — risk is on the buyer, so rates reflect the buyer's credit quality.
- Companies above a turnover threshold are mandated to onboard TReDS. Most banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, BoB) are financiers.
Best fit: MSME suppliers to large corporates or PSUs facing 60–120 day payment cycles.

- Import/export Letters of Credit, inland LCs, Bank Guarantees (performance, financial, bid bond, advance payment) issued against margin/limits.

Best fit: importers/exporters and contractors bidding on government/corporate tenders (BG requirement).

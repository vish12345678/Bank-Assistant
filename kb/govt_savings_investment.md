# Government of India savings & investment schemes distributed through banks
# Rates on small-savings schemes are reset quarterly by the Ministry of Finance — always quote the current quarter's notified rate.

=== PRODUCT
id: ppf
title: Public Provident Fund (PPF)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: investment
segments: individual, salaried, self_employed, minor, conservative, women
tags: tax saving, 80C, EEE, long term, retirement, safe, sovereign guarantee, child future
source: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=55
---
A 15-year government-backed savings scheme available at SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and other authorised banks, and at post offices.
- Eligibility: resident Indian individuals; one account per person (a guardian can open one for a minor). NRIs cannot open new accounts. HUFs not allowed.
- Deposit: minimum ₹500, maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year, lump sum or in instalments.
- Tenure: 15 years from end of FY of opening; extendable indefinitely in blocks of 5 years, with or without fresh contributions.
- Returns: government-notified rate, compounded annually (7.1% p.a. has held for many recent quarters — verify current quarter).
- Tax: EEE — contribution deductible under Sec 80C (old regime), interest and maturity fully tax-free.
- Liquidity: loan from 3rd to 6th FY; partial withdrawal from 7th FY; premature closure after 5 years for specified reasons (illness, higher education, change of residency) with 1% interest penalty.
- Protected from court attachment.
Best fit: conservative long-term savers, salaried people wanting tax-free retirement corpus, parents building a child's corpus, self-employed people without EPF.

=== PRODUCT

title: Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: investment
segments: individual, minor, women, salaried, self_employed, conservative
tags: girl child, education, marriage, tax saving, 80C, EEE, highest small savings rate, child future
source: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=89
---
Savings scheme for a girl child under Beti Bachao Beti Padhao, opened at authorised banks (SBI, PNB, BoB, ICICI, HDFC, Axis etc.) and post offices.
- Eligibility: parent/legal guardian of a girl child below 10 years; one account per girl, maximum two per family (three allowed for twins/triplets in the second birth).
- Deposit: minimum ₹250, maximum ₹1.5 lakh per FY; deposits required for 15 years from opening.
- Maturity: 21 years from account opening.
- Returns: among the highest of the small savings schemes (8.2% p.a. in recent quarters — verify), compounded annually.
- Tax: EEE — 80C deduction (old regime), tax-free interest and maturity.
- Withdrawal: up to 50% of balance after the girl turns 18 or passes Class 10, for higher education; premature closure for her marriage after 18.
Best fit: parents/grandparents of a daughter under 10 looking for a safe, tax-free education/marriage corpus.

=== PRODUCT
id: scss
age_min: 55
title: Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme (SCSS)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: deposit
segments: senior_citizen, retired, conservative, govt_employee, defence
tags: regular income, quarterly interest, retirement, 80C, safe, sovereign guarantee, pension supplement
source: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=62
---
Government-backed regular-income scheme for retirees, offered at SBI, PNB, BoB, Canara, Union Bank, ICICI, HDFC, Axis and other authorised banks.
- Eligibility: age 60+; age 55–60 for those who retired on superannuation/VRS (invest within 1 month of receiving retirement benefits); 50+ for retired defence personnel. Individual or joint with spouse.
- Deposit: minimum ₹1,000, maximum ₹30 lakh per individual (across all SCSS accounts).
- Tenure: 5 years, extendable by 3 years.
- Interest: paid quarterly (1 April, 1 July, 1 Oct, 1 Jan) — 8.2% p.a. in recent quarters (verify). Rate locked for the tenure.
- Tax: deposit eligible for 80C (old regime); interest is taxable; TDS if interest exceeds the senior-citizen threshold (Form 15H to avoid if eligible).
- Premature closure allowed with penalty (1.5% before 2 yrs, 1% after).
Best fit: newly retired clients with a lump sum (PF/gratuity) needing safe quarterly income. Pair with a senior citizen FD and a health insurance top-up.

=== PRODUCT
id: kvp
title: Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: investment
segments: individual, conservative, farmer, low_income
tags: money doubling, lump sum, safe, no upper limit, pledgeable
source: https://www.nsiindia.gov.in/InternalPage.aspx?Id_Pk=176
---
Certificate scheme where the investment doubles over a fixed period (115 months at 7.5% p.a. in recent quarters — verify). Available at post offices and designated public sector banks.
- Minimum ₹1,000, in multiples of ₹100; no upper limit (PAN and source-of-funds proof required for ₹50,000+/₹10 lakh+).
- Lock-in 2.5 years; can be pledged as collateral for loans.
- No 80C benefit; interest is taxable on accrual/maturity.
Best fit: risk-averse clients wanting a simple "money doubles" product for a lump sum, without needing regular income.

=== PRODUCT
id: rbi-frsb
title: RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds 2020 (Taxable)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: investment
segments: individual, senior_citizen, conservative, hni, retired
tags: sovereign, floating rate, half-yearly interest, no upper limit, safe
source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=136
---
Government of India bonds sold through agency banks (SBI, BoB, PNB, Canara, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and others) and RBI Retail Direct.
- Rate: NSC rate + 0.35%, reset every six months (8.05% p.a. while NSC is at 7.7% — verify).
- Interest paid half-yearly (1 Jan and 1 July); no cumulative option.
- Minimum ₹1,000; no maximum.
- Tenure 7 years; premature redemption only for senior citizens (60–70: after 6 yrs; 70–80: after 5 yrs; 80+: after 4 yrs) with a penalty.
- Interest taxable; not tradable, not pledgeable. NRIs not eligible.
Best fit: retired/HNI clients who have exhausted the SCSS ₹30 lakh limit and want sovereign safety with income.

=== PRODUCT
id: nps
age_max: 70
title: National Pension System (NPS) & NPS Vatsalya
bank: Govt Scheme
category: investment
segments: individual, salaried, self_employed, nri, minor, corporate
tags: retirement, pension, tax saving, 80CCD(1B), extra 50000 deduction, market linked, employer contribution, child future
source: https://www.npstrust.org.in
---
PFRDA-regulated pension scheme; SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak, PNB, BoB and others act as Points of Presence (PoP) to open accounts and collect contributions.
- Eligibility: Indian citizens (resident or NRI) aged 18–70.
- Tier I (retirement, locked till 60) and Tier II (voluntary, withdrawable).
- Investment choice: Active (choose equity/corporate debt/govt securities mix) or Auto (life-cycle). Market-linked returns.
- Tax (old regime): 80CCD(1) within the ₹1.5 lakh 80C limit, PLUS an additional ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B). Employer contribution under 80CCD(2) (up to 14% of basic+DA under the new regime) — valuable for salaried clients in either regime.
- Exit at 60: up to 60% lump sum tax-free, at least 40% to an annuity.
- NPS Vatsalya (launched Sept 2024): parents open NPS for a minor child; minimum ₹1,000/year; converts to regular NPS at 18.
- Corporate NPS: employers can offer NPS as a payroll benefit.
Best fit: salaried clients who have maxed 80C and want an extra ₹50,000 deduction; employers structuring CTC; parents starting long-term retirement savings for children.

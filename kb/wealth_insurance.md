# Third-party products distributed by banks (bancassurance, mutual funds, broking)
# The rep must hold the required certification (IRDAI for insurance, NISM/AMFI ARN for mutual funds) and follow suitability norms.

=== PRODUCT
id: term-insurance-banca
title: Term life insurance via bancassurance — SBI Life eShield Next, HDFC Life Click 2 Protect Supreme, ICICI Pru iProtect Smart, Max Life (Axis Max Life) Smart Term Plan
bank: Generic
category: insurance
segments: salaried, self_employed, individual, women, nri, young_professional
tags: term plan, life cover, family protection, home loan cover, 80C, high cover low premium
source: https://www.sbilife.co.in/en/individual-life-insurance/traditional/eshield-next
---
- Pure protection plans with high sum assured (often 10–20× annual income) at low premiums; riders for critical illness, accidental death and waiver of premium.
- Premium deductible under 80C (old regime); death benefit tax-free under 10(10D).
- Bank partners: SBI → SBI Life, HDFC Bank → HDFC Life, ICICI Bank → ICICI Prudential Life, Axis Bank → Axis Max Life, Kotak → Kotak Life.
Best fit: any earning client with dependents, especially after taking a home loan or having a child. Upsell beyond PMJJBY's ₹2 lakh cover.

tags: health insurance, mediclaim, family floater, super top up, senior citizen health, 80D, cashless hospital
source: https://www.icicilombard.com/health-insurance
---
- Individual and family floater health plans, super top-ups, senior-citizen plans, critical illness and personal accident covers.
- Premium deductible under Section 80D (₹25,000; ₹50,000 for senior citizens; additional for parents) — old regime.
- Corporate group cover is often insufficient; a personal policy plus super top-up protects across job changes.
Best fit: families without personal health cover, retirees losing employer cover, parents of salaried clients.

=== PRODUCT
id: mutual-funds-sip
title: Mutual fund distribution — SIPs in equity, hybrid, debt funds and ELSS (via SBI MF, HDFC MF, ICICI Pru MF, Axis MF, Kotak MF and others)
bank: Generic
category: investment
segments: salaried, individual, self_employed, hni, nri, young_professional, moderate_risk, aggressive_risk
tags: SIP, mutual fund, ELSS, tax saving, wealth creation, long term, market linked, goal planning, child future, retirement
source: https://www.amfiindia.com
---
- Systematic Investment Plans from ₹100–₹500/month; lump sum and STP/SWP options.
- ELSS: equity tax-saver funds with 3-year lock-in (shortest among 80C options, old regime).
- Hybrid/balanced advantage funds for moderate-risk clients; debt/liquid funds for parking short-term surplus.
- Market-linked; no guaranteed returns — must match client's risk profile and goal horizon (risk profiling mandatory). Equity LTCG taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh/yr; STCG 20%.
Best fit: clients with 5+ year goals (retirement, child education) and moderate-to-high risk appetite; young professionals starting investing.

=== PRODUCT
id: savings-insurance-plans
title: Guaranteed savings / child plans from bank insurance partners (e.g., SBI Life Smart Platina Assure, HDFC Life Sanchay Plus, ICICI Pru GIFT)
bank: Generic
category: insurance
segments: individual, salaried, conservative, parent, senior_citizen
tags: guaranteed income, child education plan, endowment, retirement income, annuity
source: https://www.hdfclife.com/savings-plans
---
- Non-linked guaranteed-return savings plans with life cover; guaranteed income or lump-sum maturity options.
- Annuity plans for immediate or deferred pension (e.g., SBI Life Saral Pension, HDFC Life/ICICI Pru annuity plans).
- Lower liquidity than FDs; surrender value in early years is low — disclose clearly. Maturity tax-exempt only if annual premium is within limits (₹5 lakh aggregate for non-ULIP policies issued after 1 Apr 2023).
Best fit: conservative clients who want guaranteed long-term income with insurance and can commit for the full term. Do not push to clients needing liquidity.

# NRI banking products published by Indian banks (regulated under FEMA)

=== PRODUCT
id: nre-account
title: NRE Savings & Fixed Deposit account (Non-Resident External)
bank: Generic
category: account
segments: nri
tags: NRI, repatriable, tax free interest, foreign earnings, rupee account, remittance
source: https://sbi.co.in/web/nri/accounts/nre-account
---
Offered by SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak, Federal Bank, BoB, PNB, Canara etc.
- Rupee account for parking foreign earnings remitted to India.
- Fully repatriable (principal + interest); interest is tax-free in India and not subject to TDS (check tax status in country of residence/DTAA).
- Can be held jointly with another NRI, or with a resident Indian relative on "former or survivor" basis.
- Deposits only from abroad or other NRE/FCNR accounts; minimum NRE FD tenure 1 year.
- Exchange-rate risk: funds converted to INR.
Best fit: NRIs sending savings home who want tax-free INR returns and full repatriability.

=== PRODUCT
id: nro-account
title: NRO Savings & Fixed Deposit account (Non-Resident Ordinary)
bank: Generic
category: account
segments: nri
tags: NRI, Indian income, rent, dividend, pension, taxable, repatriation up to 1 million USD
source: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/nri-banking/nro-savings-account
---
- Rupee account for managing income earned in India (rent, dividends, pension, sale proceeds).
- Interest taxable in India with TDS (DTAA benefits available with Tax Residency Certificate).
- Repatriation of balances up to USD 1 million per financial year (after taxes, with Form 15CA/15CB).
- Can be held jointly with residents. Existing resident savings accounts must be redesignated as NRO when a client becomes an NRI.
Best fit: NRIs with Indian income sources; newly emigrated clients converting their resident accounts.

=== PRODUCT

title: FCNR(B) Deposit (Foreign Currency Non-Resident Bank deposit)
bank: Generic
category: deposit
segments: nri, hni
tags: NRI, foreign currency deposit, USD deposit, no exchange risk, tax free, repatriable
source: https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/fixed-deposits/fcnr-deposits
---
- Term deposit maintained in foreign currency (USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, AUD, CAD, SGD and others, as offered).
- Tenure 1–5 years; no exchange-rate risk since principal stays in foreign currency.
- Interest tax-free in India; principal + interest fully repatriable.
Best fit: NRIs who plan to take money back abroad or don't want INR depreciation risk.

Best fit: NRIs and HNIs wanting USD-denominated deposits within the Indian banking system.

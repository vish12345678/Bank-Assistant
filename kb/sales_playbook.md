# Sales playbook for bank relationship managers / sales reps
# Compiled from RBI customer-service and fair-practice guidance, IRDAI/SEBI suitability norms and common bank sales practice.

=== PRODUCT

title: Life-stage cross-sell map
bank: Playbook
category: playbook
segments: individual, salaried, student, senior_citizen, women, nri, young_professional, family
tags: cross sell, life stage, needs analysis, bundle, upsell
source: internal-playbook
---
- Student / first job (18–25): zero-balance or salary account, RD, secured credit card to build CIBIL, PMSBY, start SIP, two-wheeler loan, education loan.
- Young professional (25–30): salary account, credit card matched to spends, term insurance, health insurance, SIP/ELSS, NPS for 80CCD(1B), car loan.
- Married / new parents (28–40): home loan + term cover to match loan, family floater health, SSY (daughter) or PPF/child plan, minor account, will/nomination.
- Mid-career (40–55): wealth/premium banking upgrade, retirement planning (NPS, PPF extension, hybrid funds), education loan for children, LAP for business.
- Pre-retiree / retired (55+): SCSS, senior citizen FD, RBI Floating Rate Bonds, annuity deposit, senior health insurance, pension account, reverse mortgage if asset-rich/cash-poor.
- NRI: NRE/NRO/FCNR, GIFT City USD FD, NRI home loan, remittance, PIS.
- Business owner: current account, POS/QR, working capital, CGTMSE-backed loans, business credit card, keyman/term insurance, employee salary tie-up.
- Farmer: KCC, PMFBY, agri gold loan, tractor loan, PMJJBY/PMSBY, APY, SHG linkage for women in the household.

=== PRODUCT
id: playbook-discovery-questions
title: Discovery questions to qualify a client
bank: Playbook
category: playbook
segments: individual, msme, nri, senior_citizen, farmer, corporate
tags: needs analysis, KYC, questions, qualifying, profiling, risk profile
source: internal-playbook
---
Ask before recommending:
1. Age, occupation (salaried / self-employed / business / retired / farmer / NRI) and employer category.
2. Monthly income or business turnover; existing EMIs (to compute FOIR) and CIBIL score if known.
3. Goal and time horizon (tax saving this FY? child's education in 10 years? buy a house? working capital now?).
4. Amount to invest/borrow and liquidity needs.
5. Risk appetite (guaranteed only vs. comfortable with market-linked).
6. Tax regime chosen (old vs new) — many deductions (80C, 80D, 24b, 80E) only help in the old regime; 80CCD(2) employer NPS works in both.
7. Dependents and existing insurance cover.
8. For businesses: Udyam registration, GST returns, vintage, collateral available, existing bank limits.
9. Special categories that unlock schemes: woman / SC-ST entrepreneur, senior citizen, defence/government employee, girl child under 10, first-time home buyer, street vendor, artisan, farmer.

=== PRODUCT
id: playbook-compliance
title: Compliance and fair-selling rules for reps
bank: Playbook
category: playbook
segments: individual, msme, nri, senior_citizen, farmer, corporate
tags: compliance, mis-selling, suitability, disclosure, KYC, RBI, IRDAI, SEBI, do not
source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasterCirculardetails.aspx
---
- Never promise returns on market-linked products (mutual funds, ULIPs, NPS); disclose risks and charges.
- Do not bundle insurance as a condition for a loan (forced bundling is prohibited); offer it as optional.
- Complete risk profiling before selling investment products; sell only suitable products — elderly and less-literate clients need extra care.
- Share the Key Fact Statement (KFS) with APR for retail and MSME loans (RBI mandate) and the MITC for cards.
- Quote rates only from the current official rate card; small-savings rates change quarterly.
- Full KYC (OVD + PAN/Form 60) and re-KYC per RBI Master Direction; V-CIP (video KYC) allowed for digital onboarding.
- Respect "Do not call" preferences and cooling-off / free-look periods (insurance free-look: 30 days).
- Escalation: bank grievance cell → RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (cms.rbi.org.in).

=== PRODUCT
id: playbook-objections
title: Common objections and suggested responses
bank: Playbook
category: playbook
segments: individual, senior_citizen, msme, farmer, salaried
tags: objection handling, pitch, convincing, FD vs mutual fund, insurance objection, rate objection
source: internal-playbook
---
- "FD rates are better at another bank": compare post-tax returns, DICGC cover, senior citizen premium, sweep-in liquidity, and the overall relationship (loan pricing, locker, service).
- "I don't need insurance, I have company cover": group covers end with the job and are usually small; a personal term + health policy is portable and cheaper when bought young.
- "Mutual funds are risky": show SIP over long horizons, start with hybrid/balanced advantage funds, keep emergency money in FD/liquid funds.
- "Loan processing takes too long": highlight pre-approved/instant digital sanction, doorstep document pickup, and in-principle sanction letters.
- "I can't give collateral" (MSME): CGTMSE cover up to ₹10 crore, Mudra up to ₹20 lakh, Stand-Up India for women/SC-ST.
- "Interest rate is high": explain EBLR linkage (rate falls when repo falls), CIBIL-based pricing, balance transfer later, and prepayment without penalty on floating home loans.

=== PRODUCT
id: playbook-documents
title: Standard document checklist
bank: Playbook
category: playbook
segments: individual, salaried, self_employed, msme, nri, senior_citizen, farmer
tags: documents, KYC documents, loan documents, ITR, salary slip, Udyam, checklist
source: internal-playbook
---
- KYC: PAN (or Form 60), Aadhaar / passport / voter ID / driving licence, photograph; address proof if different.
- Salaried loan: last 3–6 months' salary slips, 6 months' bank statement, Form 16 / 2 years' ITR, employment proof.
- Self-employed/business: 2–3 years' ITR with computation, audited financials, GST returns, 12 months' bank statements, business registration (Udyam, GST, shop licence).
- Home loan extra: sale agreement, title documents, approved plan, builder NOC/allotment letter.
- NRI: passport with valid visa/work permit, overseas address proof, overseas bank statements/salary slips, PoA if needed.
- Senior citizen schemes: age proof, retirement benefit proof (for SCSS 55–60).
- Farmer: land records (RoR/7-12/Khatauni), crop details, Aadhaar-linked account.

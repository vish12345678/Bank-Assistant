# Government-sponsored credit schemes implemented through banks

=== PRODUCT
id: mudra
title: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) — Shishu, Kishore, Tarun, Tarun Plus
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: msme, self_employed, women, startup, low_income, micro_enterprise
tags: collateral free, business loan, micro loan, working capital, new business, small shop, CGFMU
source: https://www.mudra.org.in
---
Collateral-free loans for non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises, given by public & private sector banks, RRBs, small finance banks, MFIs and NBFCs.
- Shishu: up to ₹50,000.
- Kishore: ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh.
- Tarun: ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh.
- Tarun Plus: ₹10 lakh to ₹20 lakh, for entrepreneurs who have availed and successfully repaid a Tarun loan (introduced 2024).
- Purpose: income-generating activities in manufacturing, trading, services and activities allied to agriculture (dairy, poultry, beekeeping) — term loan and/or working capital (incl. Mudra card for OD).
- No collateral; loans covered by the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU). Interest as per bank's policy (linked to EBLR/MCLR).
- Apply at a branch or online through the Udyamimitra / Jan Samarth portal.
Best fit: small shop owners, tailors, beauty parlours, food stalls, small manufacturers, artisans, first-time women entrepreneurs who lack collateral.

=== PRODUCT
id: standup-india
title: Stand-Up India scheme
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: msme, women, sc_st, startup, self_employed
tags: greenfield, new enterprise, women entrepreneur, SC ST entrepreneur, 10 lakh to 1 crore, composite loan
source: https://www.standupmitra.in
---
Bank loans for greenfield (first-time) enterprises promoted by SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs. Every scheduled commercial bank branch is expected to give at least one such loan to an SC/ST and one to a woman borrower.
- Loan size: ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore — composite loan (term loan + working capital) covering up to 85% of project cost (borrower margin as low as 15%, depending on convergence with state schemes).
- Sectors: manufacturing, services, trading and activities allied to agriculture.
- Eligibility: SC/ST and/or woman entrepreneur aged 18+; for non-individual enterprises, at least 51% shareholding and controlling stake with SC/ST or women; borrower not in default with any bank.
- Repayment up to 7 years with a moratorium of up to 18 months. Collateral or CGSSI guarantee cover as per bank.
- Hand-holding support through the Stand-Up Mitra portal.
Best fit: a woman or SC/ST client starting a new manufacturing unit, clinic, restaurant, logistics or services business needing ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore.

=== PRODUCT
id: pmegp
title: Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: msme, startup, self_employed, women, rural, unemployed_youth
tags: subsidy, margin money, new enterprise, manufacturing, service unit, KVIC, rural
source: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal/
---
Credit-linked subsidy scheme run by KVIC for setting up new micro enterprises; loans sanctioned by public sector banks, private banks and RRBs.
- Maximum project cost: ₹50 lakh for manufacturing, ₹20 lakh for service/business units.
- Margin money subsidy: 15% (urban) / 25% (rural) of project cost for general category; 25% (urban) / 35% (rural) for special categories (SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women/ex-servicemen/PwD/NE region).
- Own contribution: 10% (general) or 5% (special category).
- Eligibility: age 18+; at least VIII pass for projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing) / ₹5 lakh (service); only new projects.
- Second loan up to ₹1 crore (manufacturing) for upgrading existing successful PMEGP units, with 15–20% subsidy.
- Apply online on the KVIC PMEGP e-portal; EDP training mandatory.
Best fit: unemployed youth, rural entrepreneurs and artisans wanting to start a unit with a government subsidy.

=== PRODUCT
id: cgtmse
title: Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) cover
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: msme, startup, self_employed, women
tags: collateral free, credit guarantee, business loan, no third party guarantee, MSE
source: https://www.cgtmse.in
---
Not a loan itself but a guarantee that lets banks lend to micro and small enterprises without collateral or third-party guarantee. Available with all member lending institutions (SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis, BoB, PNB, Canara, SIDBI etc.).
- Covers term loans and working capital up to ₹10 crore per borrower (limit enhanced from ₹5 crore in Union Budget 2025-26).
- Guarantee coverage 75%–90% depending on loan size and borrower category (higher for micro enterprises, women, SC/ST, NE region).
- Annual guarantee fee paid by borrower/bank (lower for small loans, women and special categories).
- Eligible: new and existing MSEs in manufacturing, services and retail trade with Udyam registration.
Best fit: any MSME client who needs a business loan but has no property to mortgage. Ask the client for Udyam registration first.

=== PRODUCT
id: pm-svanidhi
title: PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: low_income, self_employed, street_vendor, micro_enterprise
tags: street vendor, working capital, collateral free, interest subsidy, digital cashback, small loan
source: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
---
Collateral-free working capital loans for urban street vendors, disbursed by banks, RRBs, SFBs, cooperative banks and MFIs.
- Tranches (revamped 2025, scheme extended to 2030): first loan ₹15,000; second ₹25,000 on repayment of the first; third ₹50,000 — verify current amounts.
- 7% interest subsidy on timely/early repayment.
- Cashback incentives for digital (UPI) transactions; RuPay credit card for vendors who repay the second loan (as per revamped scheme).
- Eligibility: vendors with a Certificate of Vending / ID card from the Urban Local Body, or recommendation letter.
Best fit: street vendors, hawkers, thela/pheriwala clients needing small working capital.

=== PRODUCT
id: pm-vishwakarma
title: PM Vishwakarma scheme
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: self_employed, artisan, low_income, micro_enterprise
tags: artisans, craftsmen, collateral free, concessional interest, 5 percent, toolkit
source: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
---
Support for traditional artisans and craftspeople in 18 trades (carpenter, blacksmith, goldsmith, potter, cobbler, tailor, barber, mason, washerman, boat maker etc.).
- Collateral-free enterprise development loan of up to ₹3 lakh in two tranches: ₹1 lakh (18 months) and ₹2 lakh (30 months) at a concessional 5% interest, with government interest subvention and credit guarantee.
- Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000 (e-voucher), skill training with stipend, PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID.
- Incentive for digital transactions.
- Registration via Common Service Centres; loans through banks.
Best fit: family-based artisans in the listed trades wanting cheap credit to buy tools and raw materials.

=== PRODUCT
id: pm-vidyalaxmi
title: PM Vidyalaxmi education loan scheme
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: student, individual, low_income, salaried
tags: education loan, collateral free, no guarantor, interest subvention, higher education, quality institutions
source: https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in
---
Approved in Nov 2024 for students admitted to Quality Higher Education Institutions (top NIRF-ranked institutions, ~860 QHEIs). Applications through the PM Vidyalaxmi portal; loans from public and private banks.
- Collateral-free and guarantor-free education loans covering full tuition and related expenses.
- 75% credit guarantee by the government for loans up to ₹7.5 lakh.
- 3% interest subvention during the moratorium period for students with family income up to ₹8 lakh p.a. on loans up to ₹10 lakh (if not covered by other schemes).
- Full interest subvention continues under PM-USP CSIS for family income up to ₹4.5 lakh.
Best fit: students with admission to a top-ranked Indian institution whose families cannot offer collateral.

=== PRODUCT
id: pmay-u2-iss
title: PMAY-Urban 2.0 Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) for home loans
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: individual, low_income, salaried, women, first_time_home_buyer
tags: home loan, interest subsidy, EWS, LIG, MIG, affordable housing, first home
source: https://pmay-urban.gov.in
---
Interest subsidy on home loans for first-time home buyers in urban areas (scheme period 2024–2029), routed through banks and housing finance companies.
- Eligibility: EWS / LIG / MIG families with annual income up to ₹9 lakh, who do not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
- Loans up to ₹25 lakh for houses valued up to ₹35 lakh (carpet area up to 120 sq m).
- Subsidy: 4% interest subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh of the loan for 12 years, max ₹1.80 lakh, released in 5 yearly instalments to the loan account.
- Preference for women ownership/co-ownership.
Best fit: first-time home buyers in the affordable segment. Combine with the bank's regular home loan product.

=== PRODUCT
id: aif-agri-infra
title: Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
bank: Govt Scheme
category: loan
segments: farmer, fpo, msme, agri_entrepreneur, startup
tags: warehouse, cold storage, post harvest, interest subvention, 3 percent, agri infrastructure
source: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in
---
Medium/long-term financing for post-harvest management and community farming assets (warehouses, cold storage, sorting/grading units, primary processing, silos).
- 3% interest subvention on loans up to ₹2 crore for up to 7 years.
- Credit guarantee cover through CGTMSE for eligible borrowers up to ₹2 crore (fee borne by the government).
- Eligible: farmers, FPOs, PACS, agri-entrepreneurs, startups, SHGs, state agencies.
Best fit: farmer groups or agri-entrepreneurs building storage/processing infrastructure.
